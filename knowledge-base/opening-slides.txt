TOURNAMENT FORMAT
Teams: 1-4 people, one bot each
Games: No-limit hold'em, tables of about 5
bots. Stacks reset to 200 every hand. Score is
total chips won over the game.
Clocks: Each bot has a chess clock where a
time bank plus an increment per hand.
Timeouts, crashes and protocol violations
check-fold the rest of the game.
Winning: Placement points summed over the 4
rounds decide the standings. The top 3 win
prizes, and ties are settled head-to-head.
Duplicate Deals: Each deal is replayed once per seat,
rotating the bots around the table. So everyone plays the
same cards and positions.

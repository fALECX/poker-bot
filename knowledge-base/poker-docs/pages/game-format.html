The game

Section titled “The game”

No-limit Texas hold’em at tables of up to 9 bots (about 5 in the tournament, 4 to 6 depending on turnout). Blinds are 1 and 2 and never increase, there are no antes, and every hand starts with each bot on a fresh stack of 200 chips (100 big blinds).

Fixed-hand games

Section titled “Fixed-hand games”

A tournament game is 100 hands, each dealt from a freshly shuffled deck. The button moves one seat every hand, so every bot plays every position equally often. Stacks reset every hand, so nobody is ever eliminated mid-game and going all in is a bet like any other, not a survival play. Your score for the game is the total of chips you won and lost across all hands.

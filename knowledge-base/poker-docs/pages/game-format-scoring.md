Scoring and rounds | macpoker docs- - - - - Skip to content
- Teams are grouped into tables of about 5, randomly in round one.
- Each round, every table plays one game per seat (5 games at a 5-bot
table). Final chips rank each game and convert to points: 1st gets 5,
2nd gets 4, down to 1.
- A table’s game points total converts to round placement points the
same way: best in your group gets 5, and so on. Ties share the average
of the places they cover (two bots tied for 2nd each get 3.5). The
scale follows the table size.
- Groups reshuffle by cumulative placement points each round (ties broken
by total game points), so you increasingly face bots at your level.
- After 4 rounds, the sum of round placement points decides the final
standings, and the top 3 win prizes. Ties for a prize are settled with
a head-to-head set between the tied bots, one game per seat.

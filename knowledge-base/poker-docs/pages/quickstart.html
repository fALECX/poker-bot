Get started

Quickstart

Install the SDK, write a bot, test it against the house and submit it, in about five minutes.

  1. Install the SDK

    Or start from the scaffold, which contains a ready-to-run main.py.

  2. Write a bot

    One main.py, one Bot subclass, one decision method. Everything your bot can see and do is on state.

  3. Test it

    Your bot plays three games of 50 hands against two house bots, once from each seat, and you get a chip count. See testing locally.

  4. Submit it

    Zip your folder with main.py at the root and upload it from the app. Each upload plays a validation game against the house; pick a passing upload as your main before the deadline. That is your tournament entry.

Iterate with a fixed seed

Add --seed anything while you tweak your bot. Same seed, same cards, so a change in chips is a change in your decisions.
